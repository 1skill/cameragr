## Notes for Review (English draft)

Koco is a film camera. Taking pictures needs no account. The community is optional.

**Where the community is:** open the phone flat (iPhone Duo) or open the film chamber (other iPhones) to show the leather camera bag. Turn the dial at the foot of the left page to "Community".

**Try it:**
1. Tap "Sign in with Apple" on the left column. Only the name scope is requested.
2. Tap "Post one". The community rules appear first; tap "I agree".
3. Choose a picture taken with Koco (take one first if the folder is empty), add a line, then tap "Put it in". It appears in the sleeve for its author at once; every picture and comment is reviewed by a person before others can see it (the author is not shown a "waiting" state, by design). 【待填：多久审一次，比如“审核期间我们会在几小时内处理”。没定之前不要写具体时间】
4. Tap any picture to open it. Below it are comments and a line to write one. The small flag beside the heart reports the picture or blocks its author. Each comment has a flag (report, block) and, on one's own or under one's picture, a bin (delete).
5. "Mine", at the foot of the left column, shows your posts, your days in a row and the rewards for 3, 7 and 14 days.
6. **People and letters (added 11/25):** tap a name on any picture to open that person's page (name, a short bio approved before others see it, their pictures). "Follow" is one-way and shows no counts. Two people who follow each other can write letters ("Write a letter" on the page, "Letters" in "Mine"): text only, the first letter waits for an answer, links and contacts are refused, 20 a day. "⋯" in a conversation reports or blocks; a long press on any letter reports that letter, and the last 20 letters go to the admin with the report. Letters are not reviewed before delivery; they are seen by an admin only when reported. To try it, the reviewer needs two accounts that follow each other 【待填：给审核员的第二个测试账号，或说明】.
7. **Account deletion:** "Mine" → "Delete account". A second step says what is deleted. The account then stops at once, its content is removed (pictures are cleared in the background, retried if need be), and the Apple ID grant is revoked (tried again later if Apple can't be reached).

**Purchases:** films are sold one by one or all together (the all-films purchase includes future films for sale, and can be shared with Family Sharing). Every film for sale has free trial shots, and one is free all day each day. The two limited rolls (Dusk 200, Ember 400) are never sold: they come only from posting in the community 7 and 14 days running. Three days running gives Porto 400; someone who already owns it gets a voucher for another roll, and someone who owns every roll gets a keepsake badge instead.

**Moderation:** reports (pictures, comments, letters) reach the admin in the app at once, and by email (working since 11/25). Three reports from different people hide a picture until it is reviewed. Blocking hides each person from the other.
