## Nomination name
Koco Film — a film camera built around iPhone Duo

## Description (paste as is)
Koco Film turns iPhone into a 1980s point-and-shoot film camera — and on iPhone Duo, the fold itself is how you use it.

- Closed, the outer screen is the camera: real keys, an LCD panel, and a film chamber you open to change the roll.
- Half-folded like a laptop, the lower half becomes a waist-level reflex finder with a bubble level and a shutter key, the way people used to look down into a camera.
- Opened flat, the inner screen is a leather camera bag: rolls lie in velvet cradles with paper price tags, and each roll’s card and pictures sit in the lid. Choose a roll, close the phone, and it is loaded.

The film is live in the viewfinder, not added afterwards: sixteen film looks computed from real film stocks’ spectral and grain data, plus a 2000s CCD look fitted to a real camera. Everything happens on the device: no account, no uploads, no tracking. Three films are free, every other film has free trial shots, and films are bought one by one or all at once with Family Sharing.

Koco also works on every iPhone with Camera Control, the Lock Screen and Control Center. It is made by an independent developer, with every surface drawn as a physical object.

- 合上时，外屏是相机：实体按键、液晶屏，打开胶卷仓就能换卷。

胶片效果在取景器里实时呈现，不是事后加的：16 种胶片效果按真实胶卷的光谱和颗粒数据计算，外加一个按真实相机拟合的 2000 年代 CCD 效果。全部在手机上完成：没有账号、不上传、不追踪。三卷免费，其余每卷可免费试拍，可单买也可一次买全，支持家人共享。

普通 iPhone 也能用，支持相机控制按钮、锁屏、控制中心和小组件。独立开发者制作，每一处都画成真实的物件。
