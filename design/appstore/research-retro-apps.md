Sources: Apple lookup API + live store pages (us/cn), ~50 most-helpful reviews per app. Full report in the session transcript.

Findings:
- No app opens with a close-up of part of the camera: screenshot 1 is the whole body (NOMO, FIMO, OldRoll, ProCCD, Hipstamatic, Dispo) or many cameras (Dazz) or photos (Lapse).
- Slogan patterns: an era ("1998", "千禧年", "90s"), "no editing, one press" ("POINT & SHOOT & DONE", "先拍。不修。"), disposable / develop later, "authentic / 还原", "feel", anti-perfect ("Your iPhone takes perfect photos. Hipstamatic doesn't.").
- What users reward: point-and-shoot simplicity + cute, accurate-looking cameras + one press to a good photo; manual controls are not what people pay for.
