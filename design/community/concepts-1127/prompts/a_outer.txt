UI concept art for "Koco Film", an iOS film-camera app, community screen. Skeuomorphic: the inside of an open vintage leather camera bag (dark brown leather rim with fine stitching, warm cream paper, clear plastic photo-album sleeves) - but the contents are BIG and readable, like a real modern app at 1:1. Flat front view, no perspective, no hands, no device bezel; the image IS the screen, with rounded screen corners. Design at final display size: body text 17pt, author names and film labels at least 13pt, numbers 18-20pt, icons 22-24pt with 44pt tap areas. If space runs out, show fewer items - never shrink text. Under each photo a separate two-line paper label: line 1 small round avatar + author name, line 2 film name + heart with like count. Decoration never covers text. Few, clean, legible sample words; no dense fake text. Usernames and comments must feel real but are obviously placeholders. Photos in the grid are varied, natural film-like everyday photos (cat on a windowsill, street at dusk, laundromat, beach, market). Long slim brass-edged dial bar at the bottom centre with four words 相机 胶卷 社区 设置, a short red needle under 社区. Keep everything clear of the rounded corners. Chinese UI text exactly as given.
Screen: closed foldable iPhone outer display held upright (portrait), aspect 466:678; the right two corners are strongly rounded (59pt), the left two corners almost square (8pt).
Direction A, same content as the big screen minus one level: top a paper index strip 主题 / 大家 / 关注 and a small round "我" leather button with the user's avatar; a wide photo card with label "今日主题 · 还剩 20 小时", big "它睡着了", "12 人参与", red pill "去发布"; one row with a small cover card "本周精选 6 张" and "关注的人" four round avatars (two with red dots); a film tab with canister icon "全部胶卷 ▾"; then a clear plastic album insert with a 2-column grid of large photo pockets with two-line paper labels (阿栗 Porto 400 ♡215, Momo C200 ♡62).
