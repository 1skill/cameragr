Common questions

  • How do I change film? Tap the film door on the right of the camera to open the chamber, tap the roll to rewind it, then pick another — or press FILMS in the chamber to see them all.
  • I bought a film on another iPhone. Open the chamber, press SETUP, then Restore purchases. Purchases are shared with your family through Family Sharing.
  • Can I try a film before buying it? Yes: every paid film has three free trial shots, and on a film’s page you can try it on any photo from your library.
  • How do I open Koco from the Camera Control button or the Lock Screen? In the iPhone’s Settings › Camera › Camera Control, choose Koco. You can also add Koco to the Lock Screen and Control Center.
  • Where are my pictures? In the Photos app, in an album called Koco. Turn on “Save the iPhone original too” in SETUP to keep an unfiltered copy as well.

常见问题

  • 怎么换胶卷？点相机右侧的胶卷仓门打开胶卷仓，点胶卷把它倒回，再选一卷；也可以点仓里的 FILMS 查看全部胶卷。
  • 在另一台 iPhone 上买过胶卷。打开胶卷仓，点 SETUP，再点“恢复购买”。购买可以通过“家人共享”与家人共用。
  • 能先试再买吗？可以：每卷付费胶卷都能免费试拍 3 张，在胶卷详情页还能用相册里的任意照片试效果。
  • 怎么用相机控制按钮或在锁屏打开 Koco？在 iPhone 的“设置 › 相机 › 相机控制”里选择 Koco。也可以把 Koco 加到锁屏和控制中心。
  • 照片存在哪里？在“照片”App 里名为 Koco 的相簿。在 SETUP 里打开“同时保存 iPhone 原图”，还会多存一张没有效果的原图。
