Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Support中文

In short

Koco works entirely on your iPhone. It collects no personal data, has no accounts and no servers, and does not track you.

Your photos

Pictures are taken and developed on your iPhone and saved to your photo library. Photos you open to try a film on are processed on the phone too. Nothing is uploaded.

Camera and photo library

Koco uses the camera only to take pictures, and the photo library only to save them and to open the pictures you choose. You can change these permissions at any time in the iPhone’s Settings.

Purchases

Films are bought through Apple’s In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment; Koco only learns which films your Apple ID owns, so it can unlock them.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Koco contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking code, and shares no data with anyone.

Children

Koco collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: support email coming soon.

KOCO 胶片相机

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月

简要说明

Koco 完全在你的 iPhone 上运行。它不收集任何个人数据，没有账号，没有服务器，也不追踪你。

你的照片

照片在你的 iPhone 上拍摄、冲洗，并存入你的相册。你选来试胶卷的照片也只在手机上处理。任何内容都不会上传。

相机和相册

Koco 只用相机拍照；相册只用来保存照片，以及打开你自己选择的照片。你可以随时在 iPhone 的“设置”里修改这些权限。

购买

胶卷通过 Apple 的 App 内购买出售，付款由 Apple 处理。Koco 只知道你的 Apple ID 拥有哪些胶卷，以便为你解锁。

统计、广告和追踪

Koco 不包含任何统计分析、广告或第三方追踪代码，不与任何人共享数据。

儿童

Koco 不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

变更与联系

本政策如有变更，新版本会发布在本页。如有疑问：联系邮箱即将开通。
